# UVT (Unidad de Valor Tributario)

Base unit for all Colombian tax thresholds. Published annually by DIAN in December for the following year.

## Current Value

**UVT 2026:** $52,374 COP

Source: DIAN resolución (published December 2025).

## UVT Thresholds by Retention Concept

Retention applies only when invoice subtotal exceeds the UVT floor. Below the floor: zero retention, not reduced.

| Concepto | UVT floor | COP 2026 |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| Servicios generales | 4 UVT | $209,496 |
| Honorarios | 0 UVT | $0 |
| Compras generales | 27 UVT | $1,414,098 |
| Arrendamiento inmuebles | 27 UVT | $1,414,098 |
| Contratos de construcción | 27 UVT | $1,414,098 |

Compute at runtime: `UVT_floor × 52,374`. Update the constant each January.
